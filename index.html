About me

Originally from Buenos Aires, I live in East Williamsburg, Brooklyn, and work at Spade, where I build the infrastructure that turns raw card transactions into something people can understand and act on. I was employee number five, and now I'm a staff engineer leading our forward deployed engineering team.

On the side I run Common Materials, a small furniture studio working in steel and aluminum. I think a lot about the future of payments, especially what happens when agents start spending money on our behalf.
